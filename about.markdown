<h1 data-i18n="about-background-title">My Background</h1>

<p data-i18n="about-background-text">Driven by a passion for electronics, computer science, and embedded systems, I decided to pursue an engineering career after obtaining my scientific baccalaureate. I started my journey with a GEII (Electrical Engineering and Industrial Computing) degree in Toulouse, and it became clear that continuing my studies at INSA Toulouse in Automatic and Electronic Systems was the best decision. I am currently in my final year of study and am looking for a job in the field of embedded systems.</p>

<hr>

<h1 data-i18n="about-insa-title">Why INSA Toulouse</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-insa-text">
<p>INSA Toulouse is a school that offers a wide range of courses in the fields of electronics, computer science, and embedded systems. The school's reputation and the quality of the courses offered are the main reasons why I chose to study there.</p>

<p>During the open house days at INSA, I was particularly impressed by the quality of education and the teaching approach, which integrates theory with hands-on experience. The apprenticeship program at INSA perfectly aligns with my learning style, offering the opportunity to gain real-world professional experience alongside theoretical knowledge — an invaluable asset for an engineering career.</p>

<p>Joining INSA has allowed me to explore the role of an engineer in a professional environment. It has provided a deeper understanding of the industry and helped me enhance both my technical expertise and confidence in decision-making. The balance between academic studies and real-life project applications is the ideal framework to develop my engineering skills.</p>
</div>

<hr>

<h1 data-i18n="about-apprenticeship-title">My Apprenticeship</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-apprenticeship-text">
<p>During my apprenticeship at Vitesco Technologies, I had the opportunity to work on exciting projects and develop practical skills in embedded systems engineering. Here is an overview of my main activities:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Development of generic embedded solutions for ECU reprogramming</strong>: I participated in the design and implementation of solutions for reprogramming electronic control units (ECUs), which are essential for the proper functioning of embedded systems.</li>

<li><strong>Code testing and debugging</strong>: I performed rigorous tests and debugged the code to ensure the proper functioning of the systems and resolve the problems encountered. This step is necessary to ensure the reliability and performance of the developed solutions.</li>

<li><strong>Creation of automated test environments</strong>: I created environments to automate and optimize tests using Python and Jenkins scripts. This automation has increased efficiency and reduced human errors.</li>

<li><strong>Code correction according to MISRA and CERT standards</strong>: I corrected the code in compliance with MISRA and CERT coding standards, thus ensuring the security and quality of the software. These standards are essential for the development of safe and reliable embedded software.</li>
</ul>

<p>This work-study experience allowed me to put my theoretical knowledge into practice and develop advanced technical skills, while working on concrete and innovative projects.</p>
</div>

<hr>

<h1 data-i18n="about-internship-title">My Internship Abroad</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-internship-text">
<p>I had the opportunity to do an internship abroad at Vitesco Technologies in Iași, Romania, where I worked on new projects in the field of embedded systems. Here is an overview of my main activities:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Study and implementation of solutions for Flash memory management on a new microcontroller</strong>: I participated in the analysis and implementation of solutions to optimize Flash memory management, essential for the proper functioning of embedded systems.</li>

<li><strong>Testing and validation of Flash features</strong>: I performed rigorous tests and validated Flash memory features to ensure the reliability and performance of the new platform. This step is to ensure the quality and robustness of the developed systems.</li>
</ul>

<p>This internship abroad allowed me to develop advanced technical skills and adapt to an international work environment, thus enriching my professional career.</p>
</div>

<hr>

<h1 data-i18n="about-iss-title">Why the Innovative Smart Systems (ISS) Specialization</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-iss-text">
<p>In my fifth year, I chose to specialize in Innovative Smart Systems (ISS), as it perfectly aligns with my interests in cutting-edge technologies and embedded systems. The ISS program focuses on the design and development of intelligent systems, touching on rapidly evolving fields like the Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence, and cyber-physical systems.</p>

<p>This specialization allows me to gain expertise in technologies that are driving major transformations in engineering today. Additionally, the program's multidisciplinary approach, combining both hardware and software aspects, fits my goal of expanding my knowledge and preparing for complex challenges in the industry.</p>
</div>

<hr>

<h1 data-i18n="about-reoc-title">Master 2 Embedded Networks and Connected Objects (REOC)</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-reoc-text">
<p>In parallel with my engineering diploma, I am also pursuing a Master 2 in Embedded Networks and Connected Objects (REOC) at ENSEEIHT. This program is designed to deepen my knowledge and skills in embedded networks and connected objects, areas that are increasingly relevant in today's interconnected world.</p>

<p>The REOC master's program offers a comprehensive curriculum that covers advanced topics in network protocols, embedded systems, and IoT technologies. This dual degree not only broadens my expertise but also enhances my ability to tackle complex engineering problems from multiple perspectives.</p>

<p>The partnership with ENSEEIHT provides access to state-of-the-art resources and a collaborative environment, fostering innovation and practical experience. This additional qualification complements my studies at INSA Toulouse, making me well-prepared for a dynamic career in embedded systems and IoT.</p>
</div>

<h1 data-i18n="about-after-title">After INSA Toulouse</h1>

<div data-i18n-html="about-after-text">
