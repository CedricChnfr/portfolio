### PARTIE D : PARTIE ANALYTIQUE

**Acquired Skills:**
- Understanding Cloud Computing concepts and architectures.
- Using IaaS services and deploying platforms with OpenStack.
- Designing autonomic microservices systems for efficient resource allocation.
- Comprehending the interplay between Cloud and Edge solutions in IoT ecosystems.

**Skills to Improve:**
- The course provided practical experience with OpenStack but lacked sufficient training in Kubernetes, a critical technology for container orchestration. Enhanced exposure to Kubernetes would bridge this gap.

The Cloud and Edge Computing course has been instrumental in my professional growth, offering both theoretical knowledge and practical tools relevant to modern Cloud engineering. Working with OpenStack provided valuable insight into open-source technologies, aligning with engineering values of collaboration and innovation.

**Highlights:**
- Emphasis on microservices and edge computing highlighted their transformative role in IoT and real-time applications.
- Introduction to virtualization techniques and their applications in Cloud infrastructures.

**Improvements:**
- The absence of Kubernetes training limited the understanding of container orchestration. Incorporating Kubernetes would better prepare students for real-world Cloud environments.

Overall, the course laid a robust foundation for exploring Cloud-Edge synergies and their application to emerging technologies like IoT and 5G. Its focus on innovation and adaptability equips engineers to navigate and lead in a rapidly evolving tech landscape.
