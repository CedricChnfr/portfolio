## PARTIE B : DESCRIPTIVE PART

### 1. Introduction
The "Energy for Connected Objects" course focuses on powering IoT devices efficiently, sustainably, and autonomously. Taught by Gaël Loubet, the course provides an in-depth exploration of electricity storage, ambient energy harvesting, and wireless power transfer technologies. The primary objective is to design energy-efficient connected objects that operate without reliance on traditional batteries or wired power.

### 2. Key Concepts
- **Electricity Storage**: Exploring chemical (e.g., batteries, fuel cells) and physical storage methods (e.g., capacitors, supercapacitors).
- **Ambient Energy Harvesting**: Techniques to collect energy from the environment, including solar, mechanical, thermal, and electromagnetic sources.
- **Wireless Power Transfer**: Systems enabling remote and efficient power delivery using electromagnetic fields.

### 3. Course Structure
- **Lecture Sessions**: Covering theoretical aspects, from general concepts of energy management to advanced techniques in energy harvesting and wireless transfer.
- **Laboratory Work**:
    - Design and power a connected object emulator.
    - Experiment with radiated electromagnetic energy harvesting.
    - Implement wireless power transfer systems.

### 4. Related Terminology
- **Connected Objects**: Devices capable of communication, such as smartwatches, sensors, or smart home equipment.
- **Cyber-Physical Systems (CPS)**: Integrating digital computation with physical processes.
- **IoT (Internet of Things)**: A network of interconnected devices for smart and automated systems.

This foundational knowledge is critical for understanding the energy constraints and opportunities in IoT applications.
