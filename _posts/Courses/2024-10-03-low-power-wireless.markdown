## Course: Low Power Wireless Personal Area Network (LP-WPAN)

## PART A: GENERALITIES

### Presentation
The Low Power Wireless Personal Area Network (LP-WPAN) course, taught by Slim Abdelatif, was a concise yet insightful exploration into the world of low power wireless networks. The primary objective was to understand a specific TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN, which is crucial for IoT applications. Despite its brevity, the course provided a valuable glimpse into the implementation of sensor network protocol stacks.

## PART B: DESCRIPTIVE PART

### Experience Details

#### Environment and Context
Given the short duration of the course, we focused on a few key technical concepts. The initial part of the course covered basic network and telecommunication concepts, such as scrambled networks and interference. We then delved into the specifics of a TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN, using the 802.15.4 norm for the MAC and physical layers. This norm is commonly used in protocols like Zigbee. We also explored the use of IPv6 with an intermediate layer, 6LoWPAN, to reduce packet size.

#### My Function
During the course, I concentrated on grasping the theoretical aspects of the TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN. This included understanding the 802.15.4 norm, IPv6, and 6LoWPAN. I also evaluated the advantages and limitations of using standard IP-based protocols for low power applications.

## PART C: TECHNICAL PART

### Presentation

#### Context
This section explores the technical aspects of the TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN, focusing on the 802.15.4 norm, IPv6, and 6LoWPAN.

### Resolution of Problem

#### What
We examined the TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN, including the 802.15.4 norm, IPv6, and 6LoWPAN.

#### How
1. **802.15.4 Norm**:
   - Studied the MAC and physical layer specifications.
   - Analyzed its use in protocols like Zigbee.
2. **IPv6**:
   - Integrated IPv6 for addressing and routing.
3. **6LoWPAN**:
   - Used 6LoWPAN to reduce the size of IPv6 packets for low power use cases.

#### Why
To gain a comprehensive understanding of the TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN and its applications in IoT. This knowledge is crucial for designing and implementing efficient and scalable sensor networks.

### The Knowledge and Skills Mobilized
- Understanding the fundamentals of LP-WPAN and its TCP/IP protocol stack.
- Analyzing the benefits and drawbacks of using standard protocols based on IP.
- Integrating IPv6 in low power use cases with 6LoWPAN.
- Understanding the 802.15.4 norm and its applications in protocols like Zigbee.

### Summary and Review
The course provided a thorough understanding of the TCP/IP protocol stack for LP-WPAN. However, the course was very short, and most of the lectures were dedicated to explaining network and telecommunication concepts that we, as students from a networking background, had already seen. Despite this, the course was interesting as it explained different technical choices for protocols in an LP-WPAN context.

## PART D: ANALYTICAL PART

### Analysis of Skills
Through this course, I gained insights into the principles and applications of LP-WPAN. I developed skills in designing and implementing a TCP/IP protocol stack and learned to assess its efficiency and scalability through experiments.

### Self Evaluation
