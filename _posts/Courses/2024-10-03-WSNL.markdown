## Course: Wireless Sensor Networks and Localization

## PART A: GENERALITIES

### Presentation
The course "Wireless Sensor Networks and Localization" (WSNL) is taught by Daniela Dragomirescu. The objective is to explore various solutions for wireless sensor networks (WSN). The theoretical part of this course is divided into two segments. The first segment introduces telecommunications and defines the specific needs for WSN. In the second each group of students presents an existing WSN technology from the physical layer to the MAC layer. My group, consisting of Léa Beghin, Nathan Brugiere, Maxime Valentin, and myself, presented Zigbee, a WPAN for WSN.

## PART B: DESCRIPTIVE PART

#### B.I.1. Environment and Context
After the theoretical part, we had another individual presentation on different kinds of MAC layers for WSN. The final part of the course was a project where we had to define and implement a protocol for given specifications from the physical to the MAC layer. I worked on this project with Bapthiste Henriet, Brian Biendou, Marie Brunetto, Samia Boukouiss, Yann Jobard and Timothé Bigot.
#### B.I.2. Your Function
In the project, I collaborated with my collegues on the implementation of the MAC layer. We used Python for communication with the physical layer. We developed two different programs, one for the gateway and one for the end devices.

## PART C: TECHNICAL PART

### C.I.1 Presentation

#### Context
This section explores the technical aspects of WSN technologies, focusing on the specifications of existing protocols like Zigbee and Bluetooth, and the implementation of a custom protocol for a medical application.

### C.I.2 Resolution of Problem

#### What
We examined the specific needs of WSN protocols, such as low power consumption and efficient MAC layers. We also implemented a custom protocol for a medical application involving a falling sensor and a gas sensor.

#### How
1. **WSN Protocols**:
   - Studied existing protocols like Zigbee and Bluetooth.
   - Analyzed their specific needs, such as low power consumption and efficient communication.
2. **MAC Layers**:
   - Explored different types of MAC layers: contention-based (CSMA/CA), scheduled-based (TDMA), and hybrid (Zigbee).
3. **Project Implementation**:
   - Developed a custom protocol using Python and ZMQ for communication.
   - Implemented the MAC layer and integrated it with the physical layer.

#### Why
To gain a comprehensive understanding of WSN technologies and their applications in real-world scenarios. This knowledge is crucial for designing efficient and reliable WSNs.

### C.I.3 The Knowledge and Skills Mobilized
- Analyzing and evaluating protocols dedicated to WSN/IoT.
- Suggesting optimal technological solutions for IoT networks.
- Optimizing communication protocols for IoT with respect to energy limitations and security concerns.
- Understanding the main processing techniques used for digital communication and explaining the basic structure of digital RF transmitter-receiver.

### C.I.4 Summary and Review
The course provided a thorough understanding of WSN technologies. The reverse pedagogy approach allowed for in-depth discussions on various topics, although a better balance between student presentations and teacher lectures would have been beneficial. The practical sessions were particularly valuable in illustrating the concepts.
