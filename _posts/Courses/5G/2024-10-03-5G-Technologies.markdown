## Course: 5G Technologies

### PART A: GENERALITIES

#### Context
The course of 5G technologies is taught by Etienne Sicard. The main objective of this course is to discover a lot of actual topics regarding cellular networks. This course was taught using reverse pedagogy, which means that the content of the course was mainly composed of presentations done by students. As the industry of cellular networks evolves at a fast pace, it was really interesting to discuss such a recent and emerging topic. Indeed, 5G is a big change in cellular networks as it is the first generation to introduce software-defined radio (SDR) and microservices. This totally changed the architecture of cellular networks.
