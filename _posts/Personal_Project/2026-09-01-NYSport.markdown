<div class="lang-en">
<div class="ny-section-label">The Problem</div>
<h2>Playing sport in New York City: an invisible barrier</h2>

<p>New Yorkers want to play sports but struggle to find partners at their level, near where they live. Meanwhile, public courts, fields and tracks across the five boroughs sit under-used — for lack of a simple tool to connect residents who want to use them spontaneously.</p>

<div class="ny-value-grid">
  <div class="ny-value-card">
    <div class="icon">🏟️</div>
    <strong>Under-used public facilities</strong>
    Courts, fields, tracks and parks are often empty outside organized leagues, for lack of coordination between residents.
  </div>
  <div class="ny-value-card">
    <div class="icon">🔍</div>
    <strong>Partners nowhere to be found</strong>
    No simple tool to find someone at your level to play basketball, run, or join a pickup game nearby.
  </div>
  <div class="ny-value-card">
    <div class="icon">📊</div>
    <strong>No fine-grained usage data</strong>
    Despite significant public investment in parks and recreation, little data exists on citizens' spontaneous, informal sports activity.
  </div>
  <div class="ny-value-card">
    <div class="icon">🏙️</div>
    <strong>Social isolation across boroughs</strong>
    Newcomers, students and residents new to a neighborhood struggle to integrate into existing sports circles.
  </div>
</div>
</div>

<!-- ═══════════════════════════════════════════════ SOLUTION ═══ -->

<div class="lang-en">
<div class="ny-section-label">The Solution</div>
<h2>NY Sport — A community sports platform, built for the five boroughs</h2>

<p>NY Sport is a <strong>free mobile app</strong> (iOS &amp; Android) that lets any New Yorker create or join a sports activity in under two minutes, find partners at their level nearby, and discover facilities on an interactive map. It runs on the same technology already proven in production for <a href="./Toulouse-Sport.html">Toulouse Sport</a> — adapted for New York's boroughs, language and scale.</p>

<div class="highlight-box-ny">
  A resident creates an activity: <em>"Pickup basketball at Prospect Park, Saturday 10am, all levels, 8 spots"</em> — others join, a chat opens, the game happens. Simple, free, immediate.
</div>
</div>

<div class="lang-fr">
<div class="ny-section-label">La solution</div>
<h2>NY Sport — Une plateforme communautaire sportive, pensée pour les cinq arrondissements</h2>

<p>NY Sport est une <strong>application mobile gratuite</strong> (iOS &amp; Android) qui permet à tout New-Yorkais de créer ou rejoindre une activité sportive en moins de deux minutes, de trouver des partenaires de son niveau à proximité, et de découvrir les équipements sur une carte interactive. Elle repose sur la même technologie déjà éprouvée en production pour <a href="./Toulouse-Sport.html">Toulouse Sport</a> — adaptée aux arrondissements, à la langue et à l'échelle de New York.</p>

<div class="highlight-box-ny">
  Un habitant crée une activité : <em>"Basket improvisé à Prospect Park, samedi 10h, tous niveaux, 8 places"</em> — d'autres rejoignent, un chat s'ouvre, la partie a lieu. Simple, gratuit, immédiat.
</div>
